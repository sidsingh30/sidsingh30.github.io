### About

Turf Sports is a social prediction platform built around live sports, peer competition, and community-based markets. I developed a college go-to-market strategy for Turf Sports focused on using campus events as the primary acquisition channel, with UCLA Greek life as the initial beachhead and a repeatable playbook for expansion to other universities.

The strategy centered on four coordinated activation channels: fraternity tournaments, tabling events, campus ambassadors, and tailgate sponsorships. Each channel was structured around a specific acquisition role, from broad awareness and live product demos to deeper event-based onboarding and referral-driven growth. The plan mapped 16 active UCLA IFC chapters and 1,600+ reachable Greek students, then translated those networks into a phased campus activation model.

The approach combined event design, user acquisition modeling, CAC analysis, ambassador operations, and expansion planning. Outputs included a flagship fraternity tournament concept, same-day prediction market activation mechanics, tabling and demo workflows, ambassador incentive structures, and campus rollout logic for expansion beyond UCLA.
