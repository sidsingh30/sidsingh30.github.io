### About

Sigma Pi UCLA served as a student network and campus distribution channel for consumer brands targeting college audiences through live events, peer outreach, and chapter-based promotion. As Events Lead, I ran campus brand campaigns for BeReal, Sleeper Betting, Gymshark, and Phorm Energy, using fraternity events, chapter meetings, giveaways, sampling, and direct student outreach to drive awareness, signups, and product engagement.

The work centered on converting live student attention into measurable campaign outcomes. Campaigns included chapter-wide app promotions, sponsored event formats, product sampling, and student-led outreach, with each activation structured around a specific sponsor goal such as app signups, funded onboarding, product visibility, or event attendance.

The approach combined event planning, sponsor coordination, student outreach, live onboarding, and post-event performance tracking. Outputs included 600+ BeReal app signups, a Sleeper Betting sponsored Beer Dye tournament across 11+ UCLA fraternities, 20+ participating teams, 400+ attendees, and an estimated $9-$13 CAC per funded user for app onboarding.
