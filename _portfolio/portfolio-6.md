### About

Poppy is an AI context and productivity platform built around helping students surface the right information across calendars, email, documents, messages, and personal workflows. I developed a college go-to-market strategy for Poppy focused on using campus events and student organization channels to convert fragmented student workflows into a clear onboarding funnel.

The strategy centered on three coordinated channels: tabling events, onboarding sessions, and party activations, supported by a campus ambassador program. Each channel was structured around a specific acquisition role, from broad awareness and live product demos to guided setup, integration connection, and follow-up across student organizations including professional clubs, entrepreneurship groups, sororities, tech clubs, and social chapters.

The approach combined product positioning, student use-case mapping, event design, onboarding funnel design, ambassador operations, and phased campus rollout planning. Outputs included Bruin Walk tabling mechanics, 12-minute onboarding session workflows, ambassador recruitment logic, integration setup requirements, configured-user targets, and a Fall 2026 launch plan for scaling Poppy across campus.
